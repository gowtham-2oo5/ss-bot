## 3. What the Bot Does

The Bot provides the following services within The Sanctuary:

- **Moderation**: Ban, kick, timeout, warn, and jail commands executed by server staff.
- **Automod**: Automatic deletion of messages containing blacklisted words, with strike tracking.
- **Welcome & Farewell**: Sends welcome/farewell messages in designated channels and DMs to joining/leaving members.
- **Polls**: Reaction-based polls created by staff in a designated channel.
- **Reaction Roles**: Self-assignable roles via message reactions.
- **Server Stats**: Displays server statistics to members and via a private API.
